# Custom character names

Character naming for the custom client and realm. These terms describe the custom feature, rather than stock WoW 1.12.1 behavior.

## Language

**First name**:
The required first part of a character's name. Different characters may share it.

**Last name**:
The required second part of a character's name. Sharing a last name does not imply an account or family relationship.
_Avoid_: Account name, family identifier

**Full name**:
A character's first name followed by one space and its last name. The complete combination is unique among active characters within a realm, regardless of capitalization.
_Avoid_: Display alias, nickname

**Character identity**:
The individual character to which progress, possessions, and relationships belong. Reusing a deleted character's full name does not transfer that identity.
